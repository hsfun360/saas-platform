# Class Schedule

> **Where:** Facility Management → Class Schedule
>
> **Who can use it:** users whose role includes the Facility Management module.
> The Add session buttons need the Create permission for this screen; Edit details, Save and Cancel session need the Edit permission.

## What this option is for

The Class Schedule screen is the club's class timetable: one week of class sessions, day by day, across every class.
Open a session to see its roster - who holds a seat, whether the seat's bill is paid, and who is waiting - and to enrol people, mark attendance and record the session as held.
From the same place you can move one session to another time or venue, swap its instructor, change its seats, or cancel it with a reason.
You can also add a one-off session, such as a make-up session, without changing the class's schedule.
The regular sessions shown here are generated by the schedules set up under Classes.

## The screen at a glance

[Screenshot: Class Schedule week view]

- The week bar shows the week's dates, with arrows for the previous and the next week and a **This week** link when you are looking at another week.
  Beside it a line counts the sessions shown this week and how many sessions are cancelled.
- Three filters narrow the timetable: **Class**, **Instructor** and **Venue**; tick **Show cancelled** to list cancelled sessions as well.
- The week is laid out as seven days, Monday to Sunday; today carries a **Today** chip, and a day with nothing on says "No sessions".
- Each session is a card showing its time, the class, the venue, the instructor and the seats taken of the seats available, with a **Held** or **Cancelled** chip where that applies.
  Click a card to open the session.
- Each day from today onwards ends with an **Add session** link, and an **Add session** button sits at the bottom right.
- When classes are switched off, a note says "Classes are switched off in Facility Specification - the timetable is read-only until the switch is on." and the adding, editing and roster actions are hidden.

## Common tasks

### Open a session and read its roster

[Screenshot: Session drawer with the roster]

1. Find the session (use the week arrows and the filters) and click its card.
2. The top of the drawer shows the class, the date and time, the venue, the instructor, and the schedule the session belongs to or "one-off session".
3. The roster shows how many seats are taken of the capacity, how many are held for the waitlist, and the session's status: **Scheduled**, **Held** or **Cancelled**.
   For a per-term class a note reminds you that a seat covers every remaining session of the schedule.
4. Each seat lists the person's name, the member number or "visitor", "walk-in" for a seat taken at the Control Station and "term" for a term seat.
   A seat with a fee shows its bill - marked **UNPAID** while the bill is still open - and a free seat says "free".
   Click the bill to open it on Facility Bookings.
5. Click **Close** when you are done.

You can change a roster only while the session is **Scheduled**, classes are switched on and the session is within your role's data scope; otherwise the roster is shown for reading only.

### Enrol a member or a visitor

[Screenshot: Enrol form on the roster]

1. Open the session and click **Enrol**.
2. Choose **Member** and key in the **Member number**, or choose **Visitor** (offered only when the class accepts visitors) and key in the **Visitor's name** and optionally the **Mobile** number.
3. Optionally add **Remarks**.
4. Click **Enrol**.

The person takes a seat and the confirmation names the bill opened for the fee, or says the seat is free.
For a per-session class the session fee is billed on the seat's own bill, which stays open until the desk settles it; a free class opens no bill.
For a per-term class the person is enrolled in every remaining session of the schedule and the term fee is billed once.
If a member's status carries a warning, the confirmation says so; a barred member cannot be enrolled.

### Put someone on the waitlist of a full session

1. Open the session; when no seat is free the roster says "Full" and offers **Join waitlist** instead of Enrol.
2. Click **Join waitlist**, identify the member or visitor as for an enrolment, and click **Join waitlist**.

The person joins the queue in order, and nothing is billed until the seat is confirmed.
When a seat frees, it is offered to the first person waiting and held for them until the offer lapses; the offer time is set on Facility Specification.
The **Waitlist** section under the seats lists everyone in order with **Waiting** or **Offered** and since or until when.

- **Offer** holds a free seat for that person; it is disabled while no seat is free.
- **Confirm** enrols the person now; it is available for an offered seat, or for someone still waiting once a seat is free.
- The withdraw button (the person with a minus sign) removes the person from the queue after you confirm with **Withdraw**; if their seat was on offer, it is offered to the next in the queue.

### Mark attendance

1. Open the session on its roster.
2. On each seat click **Attended** (tick), **No show** (crossed-out person) or **Excused** (crossed-out calendar).
3. Click the same mark again to clear it.

Each mark is saved as you click it.
Once the session is held or cancelled, the marks can no longer be changed and each seat shows its final mark, or "Not marked".

### Hold a session

1. Open the session on its day (a session cannot be held before its day).
2. Mark who attended, then click **Hold session**.
3. Read the confirmation: it tells you how many attended, how many are excused or already marked no-show, and names every unmarked seat that will be marked no-show.
4. Click **Hold and mark n no-show** (or **Hold session** when every seat is marked), or **Not yet** to go back.

The session is recorded as **Held** and its marks are final after this.
The button is disabled while the session has no seats.

### Cancel one person's seat

1. Open the session and click the cancel button (the person with a minus sign) on the seat; it is offered while the seat is still unmarked.
2. Read the confirmation - it tells you what happens to the fee before anything is cancelled:
   - outside the class's cancellation notice the seat cancels in time: an open bill is voided, a bill already settled is left for Finance to refund, and a free seat simply cancels;
   - inside the notice it is a late cancellation: the seat is released but the fee stands;
   - a term seat is withdrawn from the remaining sessions of the term and the term fee stands.
3. Click the confirm button (**Cancel seat**, **Cancel and void bill**, **Cancel late, fee stands** or **Withdraw from term**), or **Keep the seat** to go back.

A freed seat goes to the waitlist.

### Move a session, swap the instructor or change the seats

[Screenshot: Edit session view]

1. Open the session and click **Edit details**.
2. Change the **Starts** and **Ends** times, the **Venue**, the **Instructor**, the **Seats** or the **Remarks**.
3. Click **Save**, or **Back** to return to the roster without saving.

The change is checked against the venue, the court and the instructor, and is refused when one of them is already taken at that time.
The seats already taken stay with the session.
Only a **Scheduled** session can be edited, and its date cannot be changed here.
When the venue is linked to a facility on the booking grid, the view reminds you that the session blocks that facility while it is scheduled.

### Cancel a session

[Screenshot: Cancel session view]

1. Open the session, click **Edit details**, then **Cancel session**.
2. Read the summary: it names the session, tells you whether a court is freed on the booking grid, and what happens to the seats taken.
3. Enter the **Reason**.
4. Click **Cancel session**, or **Keep session** to go back to the roster.

The session is cancelled with the reason.
Every seat is released: an open bill is voided, a settled bill is listed for Finance to refund, a term seat just loses this session, the waitlist is withdrawn, and everyone is emailed with the reason.
A cancelled session stays on the timetable when **Show cancelled** is ticked.

### Add a one-off session

[Screenshot: Add a one-off session dialog]

1. Click **Add session** at the bottom right, or the **Add session** link under the day you want.
2. Pick the **Class** and check the **Date**.
3. Enter the **Starts** and **Ends** times.
4. Leave **Venue**, **Instructor** and **Seats** empty to use the class's defaults (shown in the boxes), or pick others for this session.
5. Optionally add **Remarks**, e.g. `Make-up session`.
6. Click **Add session**.

The session is checked against the venue, the court and the instructor before it is added.
It appears on the timetable marked "one-off session"; if its date is in another week, the timetable moves to that week.
A session cannot be added in the past.

## Field reference

### Timetable filters

| Field | Required | What to enter | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Class** | No | Show the sessions of one class only. | Type to search; Every class = no filter; only active classes are listed. |
| **Instructor** | No | Show the sessions of one instructor only. | Type to search; Every instructor = no filter; only active instructors are listed. |
| **Venue** | No | Show the sessions at one venue only. | Type to search; Every venue = no filter; only active venues are listed. |
| **Show cancelled** | No | Tick to list cancelled sessions as well. | Unticked by default. |

### Enrol / Join waitlist form

| Field | Required | What to enter | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Member** / **Visitor** | No | Whether the person is a member or a visitor. | Starts on Member; Visitor is offered only when the class accepts visitors. |
| **Member number** | Yes | The member's number, e.g. `A0018`. | Shown for a member; up to 50 characters; must be an existing member who is not barred. |
| **Visitor's name** | Yes | The visitor's name. | Shown for a visitor; up to 255 characters. |
| **Mobile** | No | The visitor's mobile number. | Shown for a visitor; up to 30 characters. |
| **Remarks** | No | A note kept with the seat or the waitlist entry. | Up to 255 characters. |

### Edit session

| Field | Required | What to enter | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starts** | Yes | The session's start time. | Before the Ends time. |
| **Ends** | Yes | The session's end time. | After the Starts time. |
| **Venue** | Yes | Where the session takes place. | Type to search; only active venues are listed; must be free at that time. |
| **Instructor** | Yes | Who leads the session. | Type to search; only active instructors are listed; must be free at that time. |
| **Seats** | Yes | How many people the session takes. | A whole number from 1 to 500; never more than the venue's capacity. |
| **Remarks** | No | A note about this session. | Up to 255 characters. |

### Cancel session

| Field | Required | What to enter | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reason** | Yes | Why the session is cancelled; it is recorded on the session and sent in the cancellation emails. | Up to 255 characters. |

### Add a one-off session

| Field | Required | What to enter | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Class** | Yes | The class the session belongs to. | Type to search; only active classes are listed; starts from the Class filter when one is set. |
| **Date** | Yes | The date of the session; pick it from the calendar. | Today or later. |
| **Seats** | No | How many people the session takes. | A whole number from 1 to 500; blank = the class default, or the venue's capacity; never more than the venue's capacity. |
| **Starts** | Yes | The session's start time. | Before the Ends time. |
| **Ends** | Yes | The session's end time. | After the Starts time. |
| **Venue** | No | Where the session takes place. | Blank = the class's default venue; pick one when the class has none. |
| **Instructor** | No | Who leads the session. | Blank = the class's default instructor; pick one when the class has none. |
| **Remarks** | No | A note about this session, e.g. `Make-up session`. | Up to 255 characters. |

## Tips & troubleshooting

- If you see a message such as "14 Jan 2027 09:00 - Venue taken by YOGA_AM 09:00-10:00." the venue is already in use at that time, so pick another time, venue or instructor; the same message can say "Court taken by ...", "Booked - ..." (a booking number) or "Instructor on ..." instead.
- If you see "Capacity 30 exceeds the venue's 20." lower the seats or pick a larger venue.
- If you see "Pick a venue - the class has no default venue." or "Pick an instructor - the class has no default instructor." pick one on the session.
- If you see "Venue STUDIO1 is disabled - pick another." or "Instructor COACH1 is disabled - pick another." choose an active one.
- If you see "A session cannot be added in the past." pick today or a later date.
- If you see "YOGA_AM is disabled." enable the class on Classes first.
- If you see "A held session cannot be edited.", "A cancelled session cannot be edited." or "The session is already cancelled." the session is no longer scheduled.
- If you see "The end time must be after the start time." correct the times.
- If you see "Key in the member number." or "Key in the visitor's name." complete the enrol form.
- If you see "No member found with number 'A0018'." check the member number.
- If you see "Member A0018 (Suspended) is barred from enrolling." the member's status does not allow enrolment; the status named is the member's own.
- If you see "YOGA_AM takes members only." the class does not accept visitors; change that on Classes.
- If you see "Jane Tan is already enrolled in YOGA_AM 14 Jan 2027 09:00." the person already holds a seat.
- If you see "YOGA_AM 14 Jan 2027 09:00 is full - 20 of 20 seats taken." use Join waitlist; the message adds "(1 seat held for the waitlist)" when a seat is on offer.
- If you see "A seat is free on YOGA_AM 14 Jan 2027 09:00 - enrol instead of waiting." use Enrol.
- If you see "Jane Tan already holds a seat here." or "Jane Tan is already on this waitlist." the person is already on the roster or in the queue.
- If you see "Term 1 2027 is closed to new enrolments." the schedule was closed on Classes.
- If you see "Term 1 2027 has no session left to attend." the term has no scheduled session from today onwards.
- If you see "YOGA_AM enrols per term - pick one of its schedules." the session is a one-off session of a per-term class, so there is no term to enrol into; enrol the person from a session that belongs to a schedule.
- If you see "The class fee item is missing or disabled - fix the class first." correct the fee item on Classes or enable it on Transaction Types.
- If you see "Configure the Bill No. numbering scheme first (Facility Management - Numbering Control)." the bill number series is missing; set it up before enrolling into a class with a fee.
- If you see "No seat is free to offer yet." wait for a seat to free before offering it.
- If you see "A session is held on its day, not before." hold the session on or after its date.
- If you see "A held session's attendance is final." the marks can no longer be changed.
- If you see "This seat is already cancelled." or "The session is held - the seat is final." the seat can no longer be cancelled.
- If you see "Classes are switched off in Facility Specification." switch classes on there first.
- If you see "Your role's data scope does not allow amending this record." the session belongs to someone outside your data scope; ask an administrator.
- Mark attendance before holding a session: every seat left unmarked becomes a no-show.
- Record a reason staff and members will understand when cancelling: it is emailed to everyone with a seat.

## Related options

- Facility Management → Classes - the classes and the schedules that generate the sessions shown here; edit or cancel a whole schedule there.
- Facility Management → Instructors and Facility Management → Venues - the instructors and venues offered on a session.
- Facility Management → Facility Bookings - opens a seat's bill, and shows a court blocked by a class session on the booking grid.
- Facility Management → Control Station - today's classes at the station, with walk-in enrolment when the class allows it.
- Facility Management → Facility Specification - the Classes switch, the class cancellation notice and the waitlist offer hours.
- Facility Management → Transaction Types - the Class Fee items billed for a seat.
- Facility Management → Numbering Control - the Bill No. series used when a seat is billed.
